# Nga Makarara

Kei roto i tēnei wāhanga he tauira iti o ngā makarara me ngā Pūkenga Makarara maha. Kāore te rārangi o ngā makarara tauira i te whānui, engari he tauira pai tēnei hei whakawhanake i ō ake makarara, hei whāurutau rānei i ētahi atu pūtake. E whai ana ngā makarara katoa i ngā ture o _Mythras Imperative_ e rārangitia nei, e tūhuratia nei i tēnei tuhinga; heoi, kia whakaata i te wero e tika ana kia tukuna e ētahi makarara (ko te tarakona te tino tauira), kua paku whakatikaina ngā ture kia āhei ai te taumata mana o te makarara.

---
## Ngā Makarara Tauira

Whakamahia ēnei Makarara Tauira hei whakaaweawe mō ō ake hanga, mā te whāurutau i ngā tauira e hiahiatia ana mō te horopaki, te taumata wero, me ērā atu. E tohu ana ngā makarara i ngā tauira tūturu o ō rātou momo: ka noho mai he ahua nui, he ahua iti hoki, me ngā pūkenga teitei, iti rānei, ngā āheinga Pakanga, me ngā Tohu Pānga (Hit Points). Kia mōhio mai, he Pūkenga tō te nuinga o ēnei makarara. E whakamāramatia nuitia ana ēnei i raro i te wāhanga [Pūkenga Makarara](0008_Creatures.md?id=Creature-Abilities).

Ko te [Mythras Encounter Generator](https://mythras.skoll.xyz/), e mōhiotia ana ko MEG, he whare putunga taniwha whakamīharo me ngā mano tini taniwha me ngā tūtakitanga e wātea ana hei whakaputa. Kua whakauruhia ngā hononga ki ngā tāurunga e hāngai ana.

## Ngā Pūkenga Makarara

Ko te Pūkenga Makarara he mea ka taea e te makarara i runga i tōna āhua tūturu, te koiora, te makutu tūturu, me ērā atu. Ko ngā manu, hei tauira, ka taea te rererangi, nō reira ko te Rererangi he pūkenga.

Kei ia Pūkenga e rārangitia ana he whakamārama mō ōna pānga kēmu, me te āhua o tōna pānga ki ētahi atu. Kāore ngā Pūkenga katoa i te kitea i roto i ngā Makarara Tauira i whakaratohia i mua, engari e whakaatu ana ēnei i te āhua o te huri i ngā makarara noa hei mea ahurei, wero, he mea whakamataku rānei. Hei tikanga whānui, me whai e te makarara kia toru ngā Pūkenga e hāngai ana ki tōna āhua me tōna taiao. Ko te pūngāwerewere, hei tauira, ka whai pea i te Adhering, Grappler me te Vampiric. Heoi, i ngā wā motuhake, ka whakaaetia he Pūkenga anō hei whakaata i te āhua whakamīharo o te makarara e whakamāramatia ana.

#### Adhering (Whakapiri)

Ka taea e te makarara te neke noa i runga i ngā mata poutū, me te neke haere i runga i te tuanui me te kore taputapu motuhake. Ko tēnei Neke he haurua tonu o te reiti Neke o te makarara.

#### Aquatic (Wai)

Ka manawa te makarara i te wai engari i te rangi, he peihana, he kiri rānei e kaha ana ki te ngongo i te hau i roto i te wai. Ki te tangohia i te wai, ka timata te manawa kore i muri i te maha o ngā meneti rite ki tōna CON, ki te noho māngere, he haurua rānei o taua wā ki te neke, ki te whawhai rānei.

#### Blood Sense (Rongo Toto)

Ka taea e te makarara te rongo i te toto i tawhiti, ahakoa i te hau, i te wai rānei. Ka taea te rongo i ngā tohu toto tae atu ki te 1d6+6 kiromita.

#### Breathe Flame (Manawa Pura)

Ka manawa te makarara i te ahi ki tētahi wāhi hei Mahi Pakanga. Ka hipokina e te ahi te kōnui i mua i te makarara, e roa ana mō tōna CON i ngā mita. I te mutunga, ko te whānui o te kōnui he hauwhā o te CON o te makarara. Ko ngā makarara katoa e mau ana ki te ahi ka pāngia e te pōkino o te ahi ki ngā wāhi pānga katoa, ahakoa ka taea e te tangata te ngana ki te Evade ki te whakaiti i tēnei pōkino. Ka tatauria ngā Tohu Porohe (Armor Points) kia rite ki te tikanga mena kāore i kōrerohia.

Ka taea e te makarara te manawa ahi kotahi i roto i tētahi wā (i te nuinga o te wā kotahi i te haora, kotahi i te rā rānei). Ko ētahi atu nganatanga manawa ahi i roto i tēnei wā me whai i te makarara ki te pā i ngā hurihanga Endurance, ā, ka uaua haere ia hurihanga, ki te kore ka pāngia e te ngenge.

#### Burrower (Keri)

He tohunga te makarara ki te keri i roto i te whenua i tōna reiti Neke tūturu. E herea ana ētahi makarara ki te momo whenua e taea e rātou te neke, e kōrerohia ana i roto i te whakamāramatanga o te makarara.

#### Camouflaged (Whakaahua)

He tohunga te makarara ki te huna i runga i tōna tae, i tōna kiko mata rānei. Ko te hunga e ngana ana ki te kite i te makarara ka pāngia e te whiu ki te Perception o ngā kōeke uaua e rua.

#### Characteristic Drain (Mimi Āhuatanga)

Kei te makarara te āheinga tūturu ki te mimi i ngā tohu Āhuatanga rangitahi mai i te aronga o āna patu. Ko te momo o te patu, te Āhuatanga e pāngia ana, me te āhua o te ātete i te mimi e whakamāramatia ana i roto i te whakamāramatanga o te makarara.

#### Cold-Blooded (Toto Mātao)

Kāore te makarara i te hiahia ki te kai i nga wā katoa. Ko te kai kotahi i te wiki he nui hei pupuri i tōna hauora me tōna makona, ā, ko te kai nui i te mīti ka kore e hiahiatia te kai mō te marama. Engari, ina pāngia e te pāmahana i raro i te 15 tohu Celsius, ka ngoikore ōna urupare, ka pāngia e te whiu –6 ki te Tīmatanga, me te ngaronga o te Mahi Pakanga kotahi i ia rauna. I raro i te 5 tohu Celsius ka noho kore-kaha, ka uru ki te āhua catatonic kore-kaha.

#### Dark Sight (Mātakitaki Pō)

Ka taea e te makarara te kite noa i roto i te kore o te mārama.

#### Death Sense (Rongo Mate)

I te taha o ngā mana o te mate, ka taea e te makarara te rongo i te mate o ngā mea ora, me te kikokiko mate i te awhe tae atu ki te haurua o tōna INT i ngā kiromita.

#### Disease Immunity (Ārai Mate)

Kei te makarara te ārai ki ngā mate katoa. Ko ngā makarara katoa kāore he SIZ e whai aunoa ana i tēnei pūkenga, ahakoa kāore i te rārangitia i roto i ō rātou whakamāramatanga.

#### Diving Strike (Peke Ruku)

Ka taea e ētahi makarara te taka iho i te rangi, te mapu rānei i roto i ngā wai ki te patu i ā rātou taonga, ka patu ki te kaha nui. He rite tēnei ki te utu mō ngā makarara kāore i te whenua. Ka whakanui te Peke Ruku i te Rahi o te patu, me te Whakarerekē Pōkino o te makarara ki tētahi taumata, mō tēnei patu anake. Ka taea te whakamahi i te peke ruku kotahi i ia rauna. Me noho te makarara i te iti rawa kotahi rauna o te Neke ki runga ake i tōna aronga (i raro rānei ki te rukuhia) kia taea ai te whakamahi i te peke ruku.

#### Earth Sense (Rongo Whenua)

He tino hāngai ētahi makarara ki te ao i raro i te whenua, ka taea e rātou te mahi pai i roto i te pōuri o te hōhonu. Mā te rongo i te wiri me te pēhanga hau, ka taea e te makarara me te Earth Sense te whawhai, me te mahi i ngā hurihanga Perception me te kore whiu mēnā kāore te aronga i te neke atu i tōna INS i ngā mita. Mēnā ka whakamahi i te Earth Sense i runga i te whenua, ka haurua te awhe.

#### Echolocation (Rongo oro)

Ka rongo te makarara i tōna taiao mā ngā whakaata o ngā ngaru oro. Ko te tikanga ka taea e ia te kite i ētahi atu e noho puku ana, e huna ana rānei. I aua wā, ko te whakamahi i te hurihanga Stealth ki te ngana ki te noho huna ki te makarara he uaua ake i ngā kōeke e rua.

#### Engulfing (Horomi)

Kei te makarara tētahi kōhao ka taea te horomi i ngā aronga katoa. Ko te rahi o te aronga horomia he haurua o te SIZ o te makarara, engari he iti ake pea i tēnei i runga i te āhua o te makarara. Ina horomia, ka pāngia te aronga e te hurihanga pōkino i runga i te patu ngau o te makarara i mua i te horomia – ko te wā e pau ana ki te ngau kei runga i te makarara, me tōna pāmahana o roto. Mēnā e ora ana, ka tīmata te aronga horomia ki te manawa kore, ka mate i te asphyxiation i mua i te pāngia e te pōkino o te waikawa o te puku.

#### Flying (Rerenga)

Ka angitu aunoa te makarara i te neke me te whakahaere i te wa e rere ana, ā, kāore e tika kia hurihia tōna pūkenga Fly mēnā kāore e ngana ki te mahi uaua, pēnei i te rere ki te hau kaha, te karo i te patu i te rangi, te patu rānei i te aronga rererangi. Ka taea e te kai-rere te whakamahi i te pūkenga Fly (STR+DEX tūturu) mō te Evade mēnā e whawhai ana i te rangi.

#### Formidable Natural Weapons (Ngā Pūtaonga Tūturu Kaha)

Ka taea e tēnei makarara te patu, te karo rānei i ngā patu mā tōna pūtaonga tūturu. I runga tēnei i te kōtuinga o te kāhua whawhai me te whai i ngā taonga tūturu i hangaia mai i ngā mea kaha pēnei i te haona, te chitin, te kōiwi rānei e taea te whakakore i te pōkino o ngā pūtaonga hanga. Kia mōhio mai, ko ngā makarara kāore i tēnei āhuatanga ka whakawhirinaki ki tōna porohe tūturu hei huri i ngā patu, te whakamahi rānei i te Evade.

#### Frenzy (Wairangi)

Ina whara, ina pāngia rānei e tētahi matū – pēnei i te toto – me angitu te makarara i te hurihanga Willpower, ki te kore ka uru ki te wairangi. Ko te wairangi e roa ana i te maha o ngā rauna rite ki te CON o te makarara. I te wā o te wairangi, me whakapau noa ngā Mahi Pakanga ki te patu, ki te neke rānei ki te pakanga. Kāore e whakaaetia te makutu, te karo, te evading, me ētahi atu mea; ka horoia katoa ngā whakaaro mō te tiaki-i-a-ia i roto i te kohu whero. Hei utu, kāore te makarara e pāngia e te mamae, te ngenge rānei, ā, e ārai ana ki te mana hinengaro. I tēnei wā, e ārai ana ki ngā pānga kino katoa o ngā Patu Tino (Serious Wounds), ahakoa ka taea tonu e tētahi Patu Nui (Major Wound) te whakakore. Mēnā e ora ana, ina mutu te wairangi, ka whiwhi aunoa te makarara i tētahi taumata ngenge o te Exhausted.

#### Gaze Attack (Patu Mātakitaki)

Kei te makarara tētahi patu mātakitaki. Ka taea e tēnei patu te kaha (me whakapau te makarara i tētahi Tohu Mahi i tōna rauna ki te whakamahi ki te aronga) te māngere rānei (e pāngia ana te tangata e titiro ana ki te makarara), e rārangitia ana i te whakamāramatanga o te makarara.

#### Grappler (Piri)

Ina angitu te makarara ki te patu i te pakanga, ka taea e ia te hopu i te aronga i tua atu i te tuku pōkino. Mēnā i karohia te patu a te makarara (i te parea rānei e te makarara), ka whakawhiwhia te makarara ki te pānga Grip ki te peka o te aronga, te pānga rānei Pin Weapon ki tōna pūtaonga. Ka whakamahi tonu te makarara i tōna pūkenga Brawn hei ātete i te aronga mai i te mawhiti.

#### Hold Breath (Pupuri Manawa)

Ka taea e te makarara te pupuri i tōna manawa mō ngā wā roa. Mēnā e rite ana me te noho māngere, ka taea e te makarara te pupuri i tōna manawa mō te maha o ngā meneti rite ki tōna CON. Ka haurua tēnei wā mēnā e kaha ana te makarara, pēnei i te kaukau, i te whawhai rānei.

#### Immunity (Ārai)

E tino ārai ana te makarara ki te pōkino i puta mai i tētahi momo pūtake. Ko tēnei pūtake (te mātao, te ahi, te rino, me ētahi atu) ka kōrerohia i roto i te rārangi o te makarara, engari ka tika pea te whakamārama mō ētahi o ngā ārai motuhake.

#### Intimidate (Whakawehi)

Ka taea e te makarara te whakawehi i ngā aronga hei whakataki ki te pakanga, hei karo rānei: ngā ngaukai, ngā ngaukai, te whakaheke i ngā haona, me ērā atu. Me mahi ngā aronga i tētahi hurihanga Willpower kore-ātete ki te pupuri i tō rātou tūnga; ko te angitu ka tuku i te tangata ki te tū, ko te hē ka tohu me whakapau i te rauna e whai ake nei ki te whakatū i te tawhiti i waenga i a ia me te makarara. Mēnā ka hinga i te hurihanga Willpower, ka oma te tangata i te tere teitei. Ko te angitu tino nui ka tuku i te tangata ki te kore e aro ki ētahi atu nganatanga whakawehi e te makarara, e ōna tēina rānei i roto i taua tūtakitanga. Ko te pānga e haere tonu ana i te wā e mahi ana te makarara i te āhua whakawehi, tae atu ki te hanga i tētahi patu.

#### Leaper (Peke)

Ka whakamahia e te makarara ngā patu Peke e whakaaturia ana i te wāhanga [Pakanga](0005_Combat.md?id=leaping-attacks) engari ka taea te whakakotahi i te peke me tētahi patu tinana pēnei i te maikuku, te ngau rānei. Mēnā ka toa te makarara peke i te hurihanga o te patu peke ātete, ka tuku aunoa i te pōkino mō tētahi o āna pūtaonga tūturu ki te aronga. Kāore tēnei pōkino e taea te karo, hāunga te Passive Blocking.

#### Life Sense (Rongo Ora)

I te taha o ngā ritenga o te taiao, ka taea e tēnei makarara te whakatau i te oranga o tētahi mea ora mā te pā noa. Mēnā ka mahi te makarara i tētahi hurihanga Perception angitu, ka ako ia mō ngā whara o te aronga, te taumata Ngenge, me mēnā e pāngia ana e te paihana, te mate rānei. E mōhio ana hoki te makarara ki ngā āhua ora i roto i te maha o ngā mita rite ki tōna pūkenga Willpower, ka uaua pea te mahi Stealth.

#### Magic Sense (Rongo Makutu)

He rite ki te Life Sense engari e tuku ana i te makarara ki te kite i ngā tukunga makutu i ngā tawhiti rite tonu. Mēnā ka pā te makarara ki tētahi atu me te mahi i tētahi hurihanga Perception angitu, ka ako ia mō ngā tohu makutu o te aronga, ngā makutu e mau ana, me ngā makutu kaha.

#### Multi-Headed (Upoko Maha)

Ka whiwhi te makarara i te Mahi Pakanga anō mō ia upoko anō i tua atu i te tuatahi. Ka ngaro ēnei ina pāngia, ina tapahia rānei ia upoko. Mēnā e noho ana te upoko kotahi, ka taea tonu e te makarara te whakahaere i tōna tinana tiritiri. Ina ātete te makarara upoko-maha i ngā makutu e pāngia ana i ngā kare ā-roto, i te hinengaro rānei, ka hurihia e ia ngā whakaoranga takitahi mō ia upoko e noho tonu ana.

#### Multi-Limbed (Peka Maha)

He rite ki te Multi-headed, ka whiwhi te makarara i te Mahi Pakanga anō mō ia takirua o ngā peka (i tua atu i te tuatahi) ka taea e ia te whakamahi i te wā e whawhai ana – ko ērā e whakamahia ana hei pupuri, hei neke rānei i te makarara kāore e tatauria. Ko ēnei Mahi Pakanga anō ka taea anake te whakamahi ki te mahi i ngā mahi me aua peka motuhake, ā, ka ngaro ina pāngia, ina whakakorea rānei ia takirua.

#### Night Sight (Mātakitaki Pō)

Ka taea e te makarara te whakaaro i te pōuri wāhanga hei mea mārama me te pōuri hei pōuri wāhanga.

#### Poison Immunity (Ārai Paihana)

E ārai ana te makarara ki ngā paihana katoa. Ko ngā makarara katoa kāore he SIZ e whai aunoa ana i tēnei Āhuatanga, ahakoa kāore i te rārangitia i roto i ō rātou whakamāramatanga.

#### Regeneration (Whakaora)

Ka taea e te makarara te whakaora i ngā Tohu Pānga i ngā pōkino. Ko te tere o te whakaora e rerekē ana i runga i te makarara. Hāunga te kōrerohia, kāore te whakaora e whakakapi i ngā peka i ngaro, ā, ko te tapahi i tētahi wāhi nui (upoko, poho, puku rānei) ka mate tonu.

#### Swimmer (Kaukau)

Ka angitu aunoa te makarara i te neke me te whakahaere i te wa e kaukau ana, ā, kāore e tika kia hurihia tōna pūkenga Swim mēnā kāore e ngana ki te mahi uaua, pēnei i te kaukau ki te tai kaha, te peke rānei i te wai. Ka taea e te kai-kaukau te whakamahi i te pūkenga Swim mō Athletics me Evade i te wā i roto i te wai.

#### Terrifying (Whakawehi)

Ko te āhua, te hanga, te aura rānei o te makarara he tino whakamataku, ka akiaki i ngā kaimātakitaki ki te mahi i tētahi hurihanga Willpower kore-ātete. Ko te angitu ka tohu i te kaimātakitaki e wiri ana mō te rauna kotahi, kāore e taea te mahi kino. Ko te hē ka akiaki i a rātou ki te oma i te wehi. Ko te hapa ka akiaki i te kaimātakitaki ki te hinga i te ohorere. Ko te angitu tino nui anake e tuku ana i te kaimātakitaki ki te mahi i te kore e ārai. Kāore te kaimātakitaki e hiahia ki te mahi i ētahi atu arowhai mō te toenga o te pakanga, ahakoa he maha atu o taua momo makarara. Ko te whakaaturanga tonu ki tētahi momo taniwha Whakawehi ka tuku pea i te arowhai pūkenga kia ngāwari ake i ngā kōeke kotahi, neke atu rānei.

#### Trample (Takahi)

Ka taea e te makarara te takahi i ngā mea me te SIZ he haurua, iti ake rānei i tōna ake, mā te whakamahi i tōna pūkenga Athletics ki te patu. Ka tuku tēnei i te pōkino rite ki te rua o te Whakarerekē Pōkino tūturu o te makarara me te whakanui i te Rahi o te patu ki tētahi taumata. Ko te āhua, me te wā e whakamahia ana te takahi kei runga i ngā āhuatanga. Mēnā e whawhai ana, ka taea e te makarara te takahi i te aronga e takoto ana i tōna rauna, engari ko tēnei he utu Tohu Mahi. Mēnā ka mahia te takahi i te wā e neke ana, e utu ana rānei te makarara i runga i te aronga, ko te takahi he Mahi Koreutu.

#### Undead (Kōiwi Ora)

I te mea kua mate kē, e ārai ana te makarara ki ngā hua o te ngenge me ngā whara kino. Nō reira, e ārai ana te Kōiwi Ora ki ngā pānga kino katoa o ngā Patu Tino. Ahakoa ngā Patu Nui kāore e whakakore i a rātou, ahakoa ka taea tonu te tapahi, te pakaru rānei o ngā wāhi i runga i te tikanga. Tatauria te wāhi upoko, poho rānei o te makarara kōiwi ora hei wāhi i herea ai te wairua, te makutu rānei. Ko tētahi wāhi (me ērā e pātata ana) e pakaru ana, e tapahia ana rānei mai i te wāhi here ka mutu te mahi, engari ko te toenga o te tinana ka taea te haere tonu mēnā e whai peka ana hei mahi. Ko te Patu Nui ki te wāhi makutu e whakangaro ana i te kōiwi ora. Ko ngā makarara mate katoa anake e taea ana te whai i te āhuatanga Kōiwi Ora.

#### Vampiric (Wairua toto)

Ka mimi te makarara i te toto o tōna aronga, ka piki te taumata Ngenge. Me whakamahi te makarara i te patu ngau, me te tuku pōkino ki te ngau kia taea ai te mimi i te toto i te reiti e kōrerohia ana i te whakamāramatanga o te makarara.

#### Venomous (Paihana)

Kei te makarara he ngau paihana, he wero, ētahi atu tikanga patu rānei.

#### Wing Buffet (Pānga Pakē)

He tino nui te makarara ka taea te pōkino i ngā aronga mā te patu i ōna pakē. Me mahi tēnei i te mahi Patu, ā, ka tuku pōkino rite ki tōna bonus pōkino ki te hunga i roto i te 3 mita o mua, o te taha rānei o te makarara.
