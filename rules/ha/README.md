## Gabatarwa

_Mythras Imperative_ wani tsari ne na buɗaɗɗen lasisin ORC, wanda ya fito daga tsarin dokokin _Mythras_. Wannan takarda tana ba da cikakken bayani, duk da cewa ba duka dokokin ba ne, game da yadda ake ƙirƙirar hali, dabarun wasa, dokoki don wasu yanayi na yau da kullun, faɗa, da sihiri – abin da ya isa ga ’yan wasa da kuma Masu Gudanar da Wasa (Games Masters) su fara, kuma su samar da harsashi mai ƙarfi don ci gaba. _Mythras Imperative_ ya dace da ainihin dokokin Mythras, da kuma sauran tsarin wasanni na kashi (percentile) da ke ƙarƙashin lasisin ORC. Littafinsa na aboki, _Classic Fantasy Imperative_, shima ya dace da shi gaba ɗaya kuma an fitar da shi ƙarƙashin lasisin ORC, wanda ke nufin za a iya haɗa waɗannan nau'ikan biyu cikin sauƙi.

An yi fassarar da ba ta Turanci ba ta amfani da AI. Ana maraba da gudunmawar al'umma!

## Lasisin ORC

_Mythras Imperative_ an buga shi ne ta amfani da Lasisin ORC. Lasisin ORC yana ba masu ƙirƙirar wasanni damar raba dabarun wasan su a fili, kuma yana ba masu amfani damar yin amfani da su, gyara su, da kuma sake raba gyare-gyaren waɗannan dabarun wasan kyauta.

Abin da aka raba a fili ƙarƙashin ORC ana kiransa da _**Abubuwan Lasisi**_ (Licensed Material), kuma ya haɗa da manyan abubuwa na wasan, kamar bayanan ƙididdiga (stat blocks), dokokin wasa, halayen hali, da hanyoyi da tsarin da ke tattare da yin wasan, gami da duk wani abu da mai lasisin yake so ya raba. Lasisin yana da faɗi kuma yana ba ku damar amfani da Abubuwan Lasisin a duniya gaba ɗaya tare da littattafan da aka buga, wasannin bidiyo, faifan sauti, AI, ko kowace fasaha da za ta iya wanzuwa ko a ƙirƙira a nan gaba. Yawancin abubuwan da aka samu a _Mythras Imperative_ sun haɗa da Abubuwan Lasisi.

Abin da _ba a_ raba ƙarƙashin ORC ba ana kiransa da _**Abubuwan da aka Tanada**_ (Reserved Material) kuma ya haɗa da alamun kasuwanci, tarihin duniyar wasan, labaru, fitattun haruffa, da fasahar gani. Mun lissafa Abubuwan da aka Tanada a cikin Sanarwar ORC, a ƙasa.

Ƙarƙashin Lasisin ORC za ku iya amfani da, raba, daidaita, da ginawa kan Abubuwan Lasisi yadda kuka ga dama, muddin kuka ba da yabo yadda ya kamata ta hanyar sanarwa a cikin aikin da kuka buga. Wannan sanarwar tana ba da yabo ga masu lasisi na asali da gudunmawarsu ga aikin, tare da ba da hanya mai sauƙi ga masu amfani da za su zo bayanku don ba da yabo ga marubucin aikin da sanarwar ta bayyana. Ta hanyar amfani da abubuwan da aka ba da lasisi ƙarƙashin ORC, kuna yarda ta atomatik don ba da lasisin dabarun wasanku a cikin aikin da kuka buga ƙarƙashin sharuɗɗa iri ɗaya. Idan kuka ƙirƙiri naku Abubuwan da aka Tanada, waɗancan mallakarku ne kawai.

## Sabo da d100?

_Mythras Imperative_ tsari ne na d100 ko kashi (percentile). Ana amfani da zar (dice) na kashi, ko 1d100, don warware mahimman ayyuka – amfani da dabarun wasa, faɗa, yin sihiri, da sauransu. Wannan tsari ne na "roll-under," ma'ana lambar da aka nufa ana bayyana ta ne azaman kashi (misali, 65% don motsa jiki), kuma duka Mai Gudanar da Wasa da ɗan wasa suna duban su jefa (roll) daidai ko ƙasa da wannan lambar da aka nufa, ta amfani da 1d100, don samun Nasara. Abu ne mai sauƙi a maye gurbin 1d100 da d20 idan aka fi so, kuma duk kashi za a iya raba su da 5 (zagaye sama) don samun lambar da aka nufa don jefa d20. Misali, 65% na motsa jiki zai zama 13 a cikin misalinmu.

Duk da haka, 1d100 yana ba da babban sassauci da dabara waɗanda ke tallafawa dukan wasan, don haka shawararmu ita ce gwada hanyar 1d100 da farko. Ko da yake yana iya zama kamar ba a saba da shi ba a jefa _ƙasa da_ wani abu (a yawancin wasanni, mafi girma yawanci shine mafi kyau!), samun lambar da aka nufa da aka bayyana azaman kashi wanda mutum yake buƙatar jefa ciki yana sa shi sauƙi kuma bayyananne don ganin damar samun nasararka, kuma yana ba da damar samun 'dabarun zar' da yawa, kamar Jefa-Jefa da ke adawa da juna (Opposed Rolls), Mahimman Nasara (Criticals) da Kurakurai (Fumbles), da wasu ƙarin abubuwa da aka bincika daga baya a cikin waɗannan dokokin. Wasan taka-rawa na kashi ya daɗe tun farkon kwanakin taka-rawa kuma tsarin wasa ne da aka kafa sosai tare da ingantacciyar injiniya ta asali wacce take da sassauci, fahimta, kuma tana saurin ɓacewa a bango yayin wasa.

## Zagaye Lambobi da Sakamako

A wasu lokuta za a buƙaci ka raba lambobi – yawanci ƙimar dabarar wasa (kamar yadda ake tantance Mahimman Nasara, wanda yake 1/10 na ƙimar dabarar). Duk lokacin da sakamakon rabo ya haifar da ɓangare (fraction), koyaushe ka zagaya zuwa lamba cikakkiya (round up). Don haka, misali, 1/10 na 63% shine 6.3; wannan ana zagaya shi zuwa 7.

## Zar (Dice) da ake amfani da su

_Mythras Imperative_ yana amfani da daidaitaccen saitin zar na polyhedral: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20 da d100. Bugu da ƙari, ana buƙatar zar masu zuwa:

d2: Jefa kowane zar. Lambar mara biyu (odd) daidai take da 1 kuma lambar biyu (even) daidai take da 2. Ko kuma ka jefa kuɗi.

d3: Jefa zar mai gefe shida; 1-2 = 1, 3-4 = 2, 5-6 = 3
